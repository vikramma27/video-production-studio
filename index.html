04 / THE PRODUCTION MAP

From first spark
to finished episode.

Deliberate creative gates. We do not automate past decisions that need your taste, judgement or approval.

01 / INTELLIGENCEPLANNED

Reference observatory

Study selected benchmark videos shot by shot. Extract pacing, staging, camera, lighting and emotional techniques — never copy their expression.

02 / CHARACTERAPPROVAL GATE

Protagonist atelier

Build original Blender characters, turnarounds, expressions, body acting and playable pilots. Iterate until you explicitly freeze each identity.

03 / VOICEAPPROVAL GATE

Narrator auditions

Audition multiple suitable voices per channel with the same script. Select and freeze exact model, settings and pronunciation profile.

04 / BRANDAPPROVAL GATE

Identity department

Curate original names, check YouTube handle conflicts, then design icons, banners, typography, palettes and thumbnail grammar.

05 / STORIESHUMAN CURATED

Story room

Build a 30–60 idea backlog from a larger candidate pool. Accept, reject, revise, shortlist and plan one- or two-week batches.

06 / PRODUCTIONLOCAL EXECUTION

Render foundry

Run local Blender, motion graphics, narration, sound, assembly and QA with a persistent job queue and checkpoints.
